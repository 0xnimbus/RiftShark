About

RiftShark simulates Riftbound matches with a rules-accurate engine (chain, showdowns, combat, scoring), card scripts for every set including Radiance, and search-based AI players. Every replay shown here was re-simulated from its recorded decisions and verified to contain only moves the engine deems legal.

Source: github.com/0xnimbus/RiftShark. Card images are the official Riftbound card gallery assets.
