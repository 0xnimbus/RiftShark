## Why the top decks win this much: the pilot, not the lists

"Expert" is a one-move lookahead with a hand-written position score (units, Might, battlefields, cards, runes). It
never plans more than one decision ahead and it has not learned anything from play. Decks whose best line is "develop
the board and attack every turn" (Riven, Gloomist, Void Burrower) are close to optimal under that pilot. Decks that need
multi-turn plans, card-flow sequencing or holding reactions (Azir equipment, Viktor spells, Irelia) are under-piloted:
in the mechanics table the top three put 4.5-6.0 units per game straight onto battlefields and cast 2-4 spells, while
Viktor casts ~10 spells and puts 1.0 unit on a battlefield, Azir makes ~12 equips with ~1 unit on a battlefield, and Irelia
2.4 units with 5 equips: their plans pay off over several turns, which a one-move lookahead does not see. These win rates are deck strength *as played by this AI*, not
a meta prediction. The learned AI (tools/train_loop.py, models/ladder.md) is the fix: each generation is trained on
self-play and only promoted when it beats the previous best head to head; re-run this table with the promoted
generation (`--policy learned`) to see how the gap moves.
